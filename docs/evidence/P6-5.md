# P6-5 — Walk history, detail and map

Every step is OWNER and requires physical Android phones with a development
build containing P6-5 and its MapLibre native configuration. No step has run;
no result is recorded. For each step, record the date, phone model, Android
version, build and observations only after performing it. Leave unobserved
steps unchecked.

Never record coordinates, addresses, device serials, raw routes, database
rows containing location, or full outbox bodies. The displayed route is the
family's real location: describe its shape and continuity in words. Never
put route screenshots in the repository. Optional captures remain only in
the git-ignored `docs/evidence/media/` directory; describe them in words here.

- [ ] OWNER — On each phone, confirm the installed build and read its model
  and Android version with `adb -s <serial> shell getprop ro.product.model`
  and `adb -s <serial> shell getprop ro.build.version.release`. Record only
  model and version. Replace any serial in retained output with `<serial>`.
  Install a rebuilt development client if the current client predates the
  MapLibre plugin. Sign in as different members of the same family.
- [ ] OWNER — On the recording phone, open Passeios with the family's single
  non-archived dog. Confirm the start action names that dog. Complete the
  location rationale, precise permission, notifications and battery steps
  if offered. Return to Passeios and start the walk. Confirm the live map
  and metrics appear, and OpenStreetMap attribution remains visible.
- [ ] OWNER — Walk for at least ten real minutes. Observe distance and
  elapsed time increasing, the line extending, the camera easing to follow,
  the bright head with a short fading tail, and one expanding, fading ring
  on accepted fixes. Record only duration, distance and a verbal description
  of route continuity. Do not record where the walk occurred.
- [ ] OWNER — While that walk is active and both phones are online, open
  Hoje on the second phone. Confirm the banner identifies the other member,
  the dog and the start time in the family timezone. Confirm the recording
  member does not see their own walk banner.
- [ ] OWNER — On the recording phone, pause and resume. Confirm the metrics
  panel dims while paused. Use system Back without finishing. Confirm
  Passeios offers Continue instead of Start; reopen and confirm the same
  walk retains its route and metrics. Switch tabs and background the app;
  return and confirm no off-screen animation replay or second start.
- [ ] OWNER — During the same walk, enable airplane mode and turn Wi-Fi off
  on the recording phone. Walk into a map area not already cached. Confirm
  missing raster tiles expose the theme background, while the route line,
  position and metrics keep working. Continue long enough to confirm new
  fixes extend the line with no network. Record observations in words only.
- [ ] OWNER — Finish that walk in airplane mode. Confirm the detail opens
  with distance, duration, pace, start/end times and walker immediately.
  Confirm the locally cached full route draws once over about 900 ms and
  remains visible without tiles. Check the visible OpenStreetMap credit.
  Return to Passeios: confirm the newest history row shows its date,
  metrics, walker and SVG thumbnail without waiting for tiles. Reopen it.
- [ ] OWNER — Restore connectivity on both phones and allow sync. Open
  Passeios on the second phone and select the same finished walk. Confirm
  matching metrics and the same full route, described only in words.
  Reopen its detail in airplane mode after it has loaded once; confirm
  both its cached line and metrics remain visible. Restore connectivity.
- [ ] OWNER — On the second phone, open a different finished walk whose
  route has never been opened there while in airplane mode. Confirm the
  metrics paint immediately, loading affects only the map area, and the
  offline-route message appears. Restore connectivity and reopen the detail
  to confirm the full route becomes available.
- [ ] OWNER — Repeat live and saved-map observations in both light and
  dark themes, including missing tiles. At 130% font scale, confirm metrics,
  history actions and attribution remain readable without clipping. Enable
  Android Reduce Motion before reopening the app: confirm a static head,
  no comet tail or expanding ring, camera jumps, and saved routes appear
  without draw-on. Background and foreground in each mode and confirm no
  hidden pulse, draw-on replay or camera easing continues while unfocused.
- [ ] OWNER — Use an expendable finished walk to check Discard as its
  walker and as a leader. Confirm each permitted discard removes the walk
  locally at once, also in airplane mode, and later syncs to the other
  phone. As an ordinary member viewing another member's walk, confirm
  Discard is absent. Restore connectivity after each offline check.
