# P5-5 — Offline photo proof reaches the other phone

Procedure from `spec/08-tasks.md` P5-5, `spec/09-screens.md` sections
3.3 and 5.4, and `spec/07-test-harness.md` section 13.

- [ ] OWNER — Install the current development client on two physical
  Android phones. Sign in as different members of the same family.
  Record the tested commit, date, device models and Android versions
  under this item when running the procedure.
- [ ] OWNER — With both phones connected, create or choose an incomplete
  task that requires a photo. Show its same occurrence on both phones.
  Confirm the sync indicator is connected on each phone.
- [ ] OWNER — On phone A, enable airplane mode and ensure Wi-Fi is off.
  Return to the task. Tap its checkbox to open the camera, then cancel.
  Confirm the occurrence remains incomplete on phone A.
- [ ] OWNER — Still offline on phone A, open the camera from the same
  checkbox, capture a photo, then cancel from the preview. Confirm the
  occurrence remains incomplete.
- [ ] OWNER — Still offline, open the camera again, capture a photo and
  choose Use photo. Confirm the camera closes and the completion appears
  immediately with the member and time. Expand the completed section if
  needed. Confirm its local thumbnail shows the uploading label. Open
  the thumbnail full-screen and dismiss it back to the checked task.
- [ ] OWNER — Close and reopen Pawlaris on phone A while it is still
  offline. Confirm the completion, local thumbnail and uploading label
  remain. Confirm phone B still shows the occurrence as incomplete.
- [ ] OWNER — Disable airplane mode on phone A and restore connectivity.
  Keep both phones on Pawlaris. Confirm phone A's upload label clears
  after upload without unchecking the completion.
- [ ] OWNER — On phone B, expand the completed section if needed.
  Confirm the same occurrence becomes complete with phone A's member
  and time. If the incoming-photo label appears before the file arrives,
  confirm it is replaced by the thumbnail. Open the photo full-screen,
  confirm it matches phone A's capture, and dismiss it. Record the
  observations and PASS or FAIL under this item when performed.
- [ ] OWNER — Repeat the offline capture and reconnect flow for a per-pet
  task with two pets. Complete one pet and confirm only that pet is
  checked, with its own proof, on both phones. Repeat from the task detail
  screen to confirm its camera and viewer return to the detail screen.
- [ ] OWNER — Repeat the camera, thumbnail and viewer checks in light
  and dark themes, with Reduce Motion enabled and disabled. Confirm
  readable labels and controls, and that leaving the camera stops its
  preview. Check labels and controls at 130% font scale.

All steps require the owner. Record only text observations and device
models; include no media, network addresses or device serial numbers.
