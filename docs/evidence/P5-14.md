# P5-14 — The living sky

Every step is **OWNER**, pending observation on the physical Motorola.
No phone results were observed by the implementer. Use a build from this
working tree. Record the date, phone model, Android version, build and
observations in words beneath each step. Do not include media, network
addresses, device serials or account credentials.

- [ ] **OWNER** With system dark theme and Reduce Motion off, open login
  with the keyboard closed. Observe for one minute. Describe the range
  of star brightness and subtle colour temperatures, stars twinkling
  independently, and the faint steady planets. Describe at least one
  meteor crossing within that minute, including its fading tail.
- [ ] **OWNER** On the same dark login sky, describe the diagonal band,
  its faint dust, halos on bright stars, spikes on the brightest stars,
  and the two nebula clouds breathing slowly. Observe for two minutes
  and describe a small, steady satellite crossing the sky.
- [ ] **OWNER** Sign in and inspect the dashboard header in dark theme.
  Describe independent twinkles, differing brightness and colour, and
  the steady planet. Observe for one minute and describe a meteor.
  Scroll the dashboard and describe the different star-depth offsets
  and their clamping without a jump in the content.
- [ ] **OWNER** Switch the system to light theme. Inspect login and the
  dashboard header. Describe the dawn gradient, the few faint bright
  stars and the steady morning star low in the sky. Confirm there is
  no band, dust, meteor or satellite, including after a minute.
- [ ] **OWNER** In each theme, complete a together-mode task. Describe
  the immediate ring fill, lit star and haptic, eight sparkle particles,
  six round motes, expanding shockwave and halo flare. Confirm the
  particles and shockwave disappear in under one second and the halo
  settles. Confirm undo produces no burst.
- [ ] **OWNER** In each theme, complete two pets on one per-pet task.
  Describe the different particle arrangements, confirm each burst
  stays centred on its badge and that the task remains responsive.
- [ ] **OWNER** Leave an animated sky for another screen, then return.
  Repeat by backgrounding and foregrounding the app. Describe that
  the retained drawing resumes without a catch-up of sky events.
  Interrupt a burst the same way and confirm it does not replay.
- [ ] **OWNER** Enable system Reduce Motion. Repeat login, dashboard,
  scrolling and task completion in both themes. Describe a still sky
  with no twinkle, cloud breathing, parallax, meteor or satellite. The
  lit star fades in without scaling, particles or shockwave. Wait at
  least two minutes on dark login to check the event suppression.
- [ ] **OWNER** In both themes, inspect the empty scenes. Confirm their
  ambient band-density skies have no planet and fade smoothly on all
  four sides without a visible box. Describe the scene and its edges.
