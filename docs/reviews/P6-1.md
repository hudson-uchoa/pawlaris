## R1 — major — A refused notification permission blocks the ladder     OPEN
**Where:** `apps/mobile/src/walks/permissions.ts:69`
**Spec:** `09` §8.2 step 2, as clarified in `22e0446`; `03` kv keys
**Problem:** the notifications step is listed whenever the permission
is missing. After a refusal the screen refreshes, the step comes back,
and its only button asks again. Once Android stops showing the dialog,
the button does nothing. A member who refused notifications can never
finish the ladder, so can never start a walk. Yet the walk records
without that permission: Android only hides the service notification.
**Fix:** as `09` §8.2 now says, ask once. After the button, whatever
the answer, set `kv.notifications_prompt_done`, and leave the step out
of the ladder while that key is set. Tests:

- a refusal moves the ladder on to the next step;
- the step never comes back after the key is set;
- a grant also sets the key.
