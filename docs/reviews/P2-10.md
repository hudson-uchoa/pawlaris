## R1 — minor — a completion id that belongs to another family is a 500, and untested            FIXED

FIXED in de29aac. After the loop, one lookup of the id outside the caller's
family: found is `404`, anything else stays `500`. The new test checks the
response, the rows of both families, the family revision and that no
idempotency row was written.

**Where:** `services/api/app/services/completions.py:44-76`;
`services/api/tests/sync/test_completions.py`
**Spec:** `03` §5 (write path) and `04` §10, as corrected with this review;
`04` §1.2
**Problem:** family B sends a completion whose `id` is that of a completion
of family A. The insert conflicts, neither lookup finds a row of family B,
the loop runs out and the answer is `500`. Nothing leaks — the lookup by id
is scoped to the family — but no test says so: with the family taken out of
that lookup, family B receives family A's row and every test passes. The
`500` was the spec's: its write path had no line for this case, while every
other create answers it with `404`.
**Fix:** as `03` §5 now reads — when the loop is exhausted, an `id` that
exists in another family is `404`; anything else stays `500`. Test: family B
reusing an id of family A gets `404`, the body carries nothing of that row,
and nothing is written.

## R2 — minor — the winner's lookup is tested with nothing else around it            FIXED

FIXED in 4f0a4d3. One conflict with the three distractors beside the live
winner. The implementer reports that removing the pet, the occurrence or the
`undone_at` predicate each fails it.

**Where:** `services/api/tests/sync/test_completions.py` — CP-1, CP-4
**Spec:** `03` §5 (the third statement of the pass); `08` P2-10 Tests, as
extended with this review
**Problem:** the lookup of the live winner filters by task, occurrence, pet
and `undone_at`. Without the pet, without the occurrence, or without
`undone_at`, every test passes, because each test holds one occurrence, one
pet and no undone row at the moment of the conflict. A wrong row returned
there puts the wrong name on a dose.
**Fix:** one test in which, when the conflict happens, the template also has
a live completion of another pet, one of another occurrence, and an undone
one of the same occurrence — and the response is the live row of that
occurrence and pet.
