is injected, so the tests feed it real-shaped `logcat`, `gfxinfo`,

second press tops the day up to 90 and adds nothing more.

## R1 — major — warm startup is left to a manual capture            OPEN
**Where:** scripts/perf.mjs:93, docs/evidence/P7-4.md:60
**Spec:** 06 §1.1, §1.2 (startup --warm), Q-63
**Problem:** `06` §1.1 budgets warm startup, but the script has no way to
measure it. The checklist asks the owner to time five screen recordings
frame by frame.
**Fix:** add `startup --warm` as `06` §1.2 now specifies:
- launch once and wait for the marker;
- then five times: press Home, wait 2 s, `am start -W -n <activity>`,
  and parse `LaunchState` and `TotalTime`;
- fail a run whose `LaunchState` is `COLD`;
- report the median against `< 500 ms`.

Cover the parser and the cold-run rule with tests. Replace the
checklist's recording steps with the command.
