## R1 — minor — a double tap on Salvar can record two weights           OPEN

**Where:** `apps/mobile/src/features/pets/WeightFormScreen.tsx:60`
**Spec:** `AGENTS.md` §3.5; `09` §4.3
**Problem:** this is P4-5's R2 again, on the weight form. `finished`
stops a second **Salvar** before the route closes, and each save
generates its own entry id, so without the guard a double tap would
record the weight twice. Removing the guard survives (`double save`).
**Fix:** press `weight-save` twice with a valid value. Assert one outbox
entry, one weight in the store and one `onClose`.

## R2 — minor — an upcoming care row says "não feito"                   OPEN

**Where:** `apps/mobile/src/features/pets/PetProfileScreen.tsx:67`
**Spec:** `09` §4.3 section 3; `09` §5.3 (where `não feito` belongs)
**Problem:** every occurrence without a completion is labelled `não
feito`, including one due later today. `09` uses `não feito` only in a
task's history, for an occurrence whose day has passed. On the profile
it would tell a person that the evening dose was missed at 9 in the
morning. The cross-check of R1.19 needs who gave what and when; a row
not yet done needs no label.
**Fix:** an occurrence without a completion shows its time and no
completion label. Add a case with one done and one later occurrence:
the done row reads `<name> · HH:mm`, and the later one has no `não
feito`.
