## What was checked and holds

- **Boundary:** under `spec/` only the checkbox and an appended
  `QUESTIONS.md` entry changed. The fixtures were untouched.
- **Task reminders come from the day view.** The planner asks `buildDayView`
  for each of the eight days from today to today + 7 in scope `mine`, and
  keeps the incomplete, timed occurrences dated on that day. So a reminder
  exists exactly when the dashboard shows the occurrence as not done:
  assignment, archived pets, undone completions, `per_pet` progress and a
  predecessor's completion counting for its successor are decided in one
  place. Carry-overs are left out by their original date.
- **Fire times** are the slot instant, 09:00 family time for a health due,
  and `ends_at` for a timer; only instants after `now` are planned.
- **The cap** keeps the 200 soonest task reminders, after suppression.
- **`fp`** is FNV-1a over the item's other fields with sorted keys: it moves
  when the title, a pet's name, the channel or the fire time moves, and not
  when only `revision` or `updated_at` do.

## R1 — minor — an archived pet still gets its health reminder          OPEN

**Where:** `packages/shared/src/reminders.ts:84-94`;
`packages/shared/src/reminders.test.ts:196-199`
**Spec:** `02` R2.2 and R2.11, `07` §4.4 RM-8 and `09` §9.3 as changed with
this review; ADR-035; the answer to Q-10
**Problem:** a health due is planned whatever the state of its pet, as Q-10
assumed from R2.11. The spec was silent, and the owner decided on 2026-10-04
that an archived pet has no health reminders. This is a change of the spec,
not a defect of the implementation.
**Fix:** plan a health due only when its pet is in the replica and is neither
archived nor soft-deleted. RM-8 then expects nothing for an archived pet and
nothing for an event whose pet is absent — the two assertions that today
expect a planned item change with the rule — and `petName` is always present
on a health due.

FIXED in e7a2e05 — RM-8 rejects absent, archived and soft-deleted pets and
checks that unarchiving restores the reminder. The changed assertion failed
before the fix. The reminder gate passes 12 tests; full verification reports
13 pass, 0 fail, 1 pending.
