## R1 — major — a fork restarts the interval                            OPEN

**Where:** `packages/shared/src/taskEdit.ts:68`
**Spec:** `02` R3.8 and `07` §4.3 as changed with this review; ADR-034
**Problem:** the successor starts on `E`, as R3.8 said. An interval is counted
from `starts_on`, so every schedule edit of a template with an interval above
1 restarts its cycle. Measured with the code under review, the edit changing
only `times_of_day`:

| Old template | `E` | Old cycle | Successor's occurrences |
|---|---|---|---|
| every 3 days from 10-01 | 10-05 | 10-04, 10-07, 10-10 | 10-05, 10-08, 10-11 |
| every 2 weeks on Monday from 09-28 | 10-07 | 10-12, 10-26 | 10-19, 11-02 |
| every 3 months on the 5th from 08-05 | 10-07 | 11-05, 02-05 | 01-05 |

A dose given on the 4th comes due again on the 5th; a dose due in November
moves to January. This is a defect of the spec, not of the implementation.
**Fix:** compute the successor's `starts_on` as `07` §4.3 now states under
"The successor's start, precisely": the `once` date; `E` when the edit
changes `freq` or `interval`; otherwise the first day on or after `E` on the
old cycle. Tests FK-10 to FK-13, with the dates given in the table of cases,
written first and seen to fail. FK-6 and FK-9 keep passing unchanged: with an
interval of 1, and with `E` equal to the old `starts_on`, the start is `E`.

## R2 — minor — the same pets or days in another order fork the task     OPEN

**Where:** `packages/shared/src/taskEdit.ts:27-30`
**Spec:** the answer to Q-9; `07` §4.3 FK-1
**Problem:** arrays are compared in order. `pet_ids` `[b, a]` against
`[a, b]`, `byday` `[WE, MO]` against `[MO, WE]`, and `bymonthday` `[15, 1]`
against `[1, 15]` all classify as `schedule`. Nothing in `03` §4.1 fixes the
order of these three, so a form that rebuilds the list from its toggles would
fork a task whose title alone was edited — a second template and an entry in
Encerradas for no change.
**Fix:** compare `pet_ids`, `byday` and `bymonthday` as sets. `times_of_day`
is validated as sorted and stays compared in order. FK-1 asserts the three
reorderings are `cosmetic`; FK-2 keeps a changed member as `schedule`.

## R3 — minor — `buildEnd` can move an end date later                     OPEN

**Where:** `packages/shared/src/taskEdit.ts:77-80`
**Spec:** the answer to Q-9; `07` §4.3 FK-8; ADR-016
**Problem:** `buildEnd(old, E)` ignores `old`. For a template that already
ended on 10-03, ending or forking it with `E` = 10-07 returns
`{ends_on: 10-06}`: three days of occurrences that never existed appear in
the past, all overdue. History is not to be rewritten.
**Fix:** return the earlier of `E − 1` and `old.ends_on` when the latter is
set. FK-8 asserts both orders. The `void _old` goes away with it.
