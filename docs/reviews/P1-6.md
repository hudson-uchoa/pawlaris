## R1 — minor — a dog standing still shows a speed                      FIXED

FIXED in 210f7ec. The standing-jitter and bent-path assertions failed on
path speed before the fix. Speed now measures displacement with the 5 m
floor; the window, accuracy filter and pause boundary remain covered.

**Where:** `packages/shared/src/walk.ts:66-85`;
`packages/shared/src/walk.test.ts:144-165`
**Spec:** `02` R4.12 and `07` §4.5 as changed with this review; the answer
to Q-11
**Problem:** `currentSpeedMps` adds up the path between consecutive fixes of
the last 10 s. GPS jitter is path too. Simulated with the code under review,
a fix every 3 s and no movement at all:

| Jitter around the spot | Distance counted | Speed shown, mean | Speed shown, peak |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 m | 0 m | 1.9 km/h | 3.3 km/h |
| 3 m | 0 m | 2.6 km/h | 3.6 km/h |
| 4 m | 0 m | 4.1 km/h | 5.5 km/h |

The distance is right — R4.11 gives it a 5 m floor against exactly this — and
the speed beside it says the dog is walking. The spec said only "over the
last 10 s of good points"; Q-11 asked, and this is the answer.
**Fix:** measure displacement, not path, with the same floor, as `07` §4.5
now states: the straight line between the oldest and the newest good fix of
the window, divided by their time difference, and 0 when that line is
shorter than 5 m. The window and the pause rule stay as they are. Tests: the
three cases in `07` §4.5, the standing one written first and seen to fail.

  no fix arrives at all would leave the position in place and count the
  segment across it. Recorded in the P6-2 entry of `08`: `pause()` writes one
