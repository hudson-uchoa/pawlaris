## R1 — major — a reseed can delete the family                      OPEN

**Where:** `services/api/app/services/reseed.py`, `merged_values`
**Spec:** `11` §3.1 — "`family`: `name` and `timezone` only"
**Problem:** the tombstone rule runs for every row that has a `deleted_at`
key, and the family row has one. A member sends the family row with
`deleted_at` set and the server stores it (seen by the reviewer: `200`,
`updated: 1`, `family.deleted_at` set). Nothing deletes a family, and by
RS-3 that tombstone could never be lifted.
**Fix:** for `family`, merge `name` and `timezone` and the change time, and
nothing else. Vector `a-family-is-never-deleted`.

## R2 — major — a walk says it has a route the server lacks         OPEN

**Where:** `services/api/app/services/reseed.py`, `WALK_METRICS`, and the
insert in `merge_row`
**Spec:** `11` §3.1, `walk_sessions` and `walk_routes` — made explicit with
this review: the spec said only that a route "sets `has_route` and
`point_count`"
**Problem:** `has_route` and `point_count` are copied from the incoming walk
row. A phone that did not record a walk holds its row and not its route —
the usual case — so the server ends with `has_route: true` and no route:
`GET /walks/{id}/route` answers `404` for a walk whose row promises one
(seen by the reviewer). The other way round, a walk row with `has_route:
false` clears the flag of a route the server does store.
**Fix:** never take the two from a `walk_sessions` row. A walk that arrives
is stored with `false` and `0`; a walk already stored keeps its own; only a
`walk_routes` row sets them. Vectors
`a-finished-walk-arrives-without-its-route`,
`a-walk-and-its-route-arrive-together` and
`a-walk-row-never-clears-a-stored-route`.

## R3 — minor — a finished walk can lose its note                   OPEN

**Where:** `services/api/app/services/reseed.py`, `EDITABLE_FIELDS`
**Spec:** `11` §3.1, `walk_sessions` — made explicit with this review: "the
status, the metrics and the note — everything `finish` writes"
**Problem:** `note` follows the newer `updated_at` while the metrics follow
the further status. An `active` row with a later instant than the finished
one keeps the server's metrics and replaces its note with null.
**Fix:** the note goes with the metrics. Vector
`walk-the-further-status-wins` now carries a note.

## R4 — minor — a stub's colour is any string                       OPEN

**Where:** `services/api/app/schemas/reseed.py`, `ReseedMember.color`
**Spec:** `03` §2 — `color` is one of the eight identity keys; `11` §3 now
says a `members` row is checked for it
**Problem:** `color: str` accepts any text of any length, and a stub stores
it. The app maps a key to a shade and has none for anything else.
**Fix:** accept the eight keys only, `422` otherwise; one test. The contract
changes, so freeze it and regenerate the types in the same commit.

## R5 — minor — two rules that no test holds                        OPEN
