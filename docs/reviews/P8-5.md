## R1 — major — the APK misses its budget   FIXED in 1f946b3
**Where:** apps/mobile/app.config.ts:72
**Spec:** 06 §1.1 (release APK at most 60 MB), 08 P8-5
**Problem:** the APK is 67.55 MB. R8 did its part: the three dex files
are about 8 MB. But `lib/` holds 50.3 MB, stored uncompressed.
**Fix:** set `useLegacyPackaging: true` in the `android` block of
`expo-build-properties`, which compresses the native libraries. Do not
exclude any library: nothing here can prove on a phone that it is
unused. The orchestrator rebuilds to check the number.

## R2 — major — the checklist does not cover what R8 can break   FIXED in 90bdda8
**Where:** docs/evidence/P8-5.md
**Spec:** 07 §13, 08 P8-5
**Problem:** R8 failures appear only at runtime, in a class something
looks up by name, and this is the first build with R8 on. The checklist
would only open the app and sync. Its size step also names no APK path.
**Fix:** give exact commands, and add a step that uses each native
area once on the signed APK: the map on a walk, the camera for a photo
proof, a local reminder, the sky on the dashboard, and an offline
restart. A crash there is a failure, with its `adb logcat` tail
recorded. Use `node scripts/perf.mjs apk --apk <path>`.
