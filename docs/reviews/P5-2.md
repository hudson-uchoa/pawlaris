## R1 — minor — a member with a stored leader scope is untested         OPEN

**Where:** `apps/mobile/src/features/dashboard/DashboardScreen.tsx:93`
**Spec:** R6.11 (a member sees only their own tasks and the unassigned
ones)
**Problem:** `kv.scope_filter` outlives a change of role. A leader who
chose Todas and is later made a member still has `all` stored. The only
thing keeping R6.11 is line 93, which ignores the stored value for a
member. The mutant that drops it survives, because the member cases
start with an empty `kv`.
**Fix:** a case that stores `scope_filter = 'all'` (and one with
`user:<other id>`), renders as a member, and asserts that another
member's task is absent while their own and the unassigned ones show.

## R2 — minor — the day clamp after midnight is untested                OPEN

**Where:** `apps/mobile/src/features/dashboard/DashboardScreen.tsx:90-91`
**Spec:** R6.2 (−90 / +30 days from today)
**Problem:** a selected day can leave the window only when `today`
moves: viewing the 90th day back, midnight passes, and that day becomes
the 91st. The clamp handles it. The mutant that removes the clamp
survives, because no case advances the clock across midnight while on
a limit day.
**Fix:** a case that goes back to the −90 limit, advances the injected
clock past the family's midnight with a minute tick, and asserts that
the header shows the new −90 day with `day-prev` disabled.
