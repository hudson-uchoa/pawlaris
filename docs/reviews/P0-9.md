## Device

Five of the six screenshots were inspected by the reviewer and show what
`06` §4.11 defines:

- the colour icon in the launcher, on the Motorola and on the Samsung
- the one-colour mark with themed icons on, on the Motorola
- the mark on the night sky at cold start, on both phones

Not run: **the themed icon on the Samsung.** One UI only tints icons with
its default icon set, and this phone uses an icon pack. Switching it warns
that apps may close and unsaved data be lost, so the implementer stopped at
the confirmation and left the phone as it was — the right call on someone
else's phone. The owner decided on 2026-10-03 not to run it: the one-colour
layer is the same file on both phones and was seen working on the Motorola.
The task's Device line now asks for the themed icon on one phone, and is
checked.
