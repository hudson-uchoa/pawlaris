## R1 — minor — The per-pet burst seed is untested            OPEN
**Where:** `apps/mobile/src/features/dashboard/PetToggle.tsx:31`,
`TaskCard.tsx:167`   **Spec:** `06` §4.2, Q-52
**Problem:** `06` §4.2 seeds a burst with the occurrence key, with the
pet's id appended for a per-pet toggle, so two pets of one card never
burst alike. Dropping `+ pet.id` survives every test. So does removing
the together card's `occurrenceKey`, which falls back to the testID and
seeds from a string the spec does not name.
**Fix:** a dashboard test that completes two pets of one per-pet card
and asserts their bursts differ, and that the together card's burst
equals `generateStarBurst(occurrenceKey)`.

## R2 — minor — A meteor outside its window is never checked   OPEN
**Where:** `apps/mobile/src/ui/cosmos/SkyEvents.tsx:53`
**Spec:** `06` §4.1 (events)
**Problem:** a mutant that keeps the meteor's opacity at 1 between
events, so a static streak sits on the sky, passes every test. The
same holds for the satellite (line 66).
**Fix:** a test on an animated dark sky that reads both events'
opacities before the first scheduled start, during its window, and
after its duration: 0, then visible, then 0.

## R3 — minor — A resumed clock may jump, untested           OPEN
**Where:** `apps/mobile/src/ui/cosmos/skyClock.ts:20`
**Spec:** `06` §5 (ambient effects stop off screen); the evidence's
"resumes without a catch-up of sky events"
**Problem:** the clock adds `timestamp - previousFrame` each frame, and
`onStart` seeds `previousFrame` from the start timestamp. Setting it to
0 there instead adds the whole time since boot on the first frame after
a resume, which jumps every twinkle and fires a burst of skipped events.
That mutant survives: the lifecycle tests check that the clock freezes,
not that it resumes from where it stopped.
**Fix:** extend the focus test: after blur, a wait, and focus, the
first frames advance the clock by about one frame, not by the time
spent away.
