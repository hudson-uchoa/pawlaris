- **Red before green, seen by the reviewer:** on the tree of `11c9bd8`,

  With `093effd` that last one passed: 23 failed, 3 passed.
- **The green stage changed no test.**
- **On a real Uvicorn** (run by the reviewer, with a real client): the
  upgrade is accepted; no `auth` frame closes with 4408 after five seconds;
  a bad token, bad JSON and a `ping` as first frame close with 4401 at once;
  a token in the URL authenticates nobody.
- **Order at `auth`:** the socket is registered in the hub, then the family
  revision is read, then `ready` is sent. Swapping the two fails a test.
- **Authentication:** the token comes from the frame only; an expired token,
  one that does not verify, and the token of a disabled member close with
  4401.
- **Expiry, against the injected Clock:** on the next inbound frame, before
  an outbound poke (the poke is not delivered) and by the sweep alone, each
  closes with 4401; a token is expired at the instant of its `exp`. The
  sweep task starts with the app and is cancelled and awaited when it stops.
- **`ready` and `pong`** carry the family's current revision; a `pong` after
  a mutation carries the new one.
- **Pokes:** a mutation by one member pokes both members' sockets with the
  revision after the commit, and no socket of another family. The poke is an
  after-commit callback: a fresh connection opened inside it already sees
  the row and the revision; a failed commit, a `422` and a replayed
  `Idempotency-Key` poke nobody; a poke that raises leaves the response as
  it was. The wrapper registers it for every ⟳ route; `PATCH /me`,
  `/auth/redeem` and `PUT /assets/{id}` register it themselves. No other
  writer outside the wrapper changes a synced row.
- **The revision in the poke** is read inside the transaction, after its
  last write and under the family lock, so it is the revision the commit
  publishes. One short query per mutation.
- **No connection is held while the socket waits:** the user, and the
  revision for `ready` and for each `pong`, are read in sessions that are
  closed before the next frame is awaited.
- **Census:** exactly one WebSocket route, `/ws`; none, another path or two
  of them fail the census test.
- **Mutation:** 22 mutants of the hub, the route and the poke registrations
  against the socket tests: 16 killed. Of the six survivors, five are rules
  no test holds (R3); the sixth, an inbound `ping` that does not check
  expiry itself, is caught by the check before its `pong`.

## R1 — minor — a phone that goes away is logged as an error         OPEN

**Where:** `services/api/app/realtime.py` (`Hub.send`, `Hub.sweep`)
**Spec:** `05` §7 (logs), as extended with this review
**Problem:** when a frame cannot be delivered the hub calls
`logging.exception(...)`: a plain-text line followed by a traceback, at
`ERROR`, among logs that are one JSON object per line. The usual cause is a
phone that left without closing — the most ordinary event a socket has — so
the log would fill with tracebacks that mean nothing, in a shape the rest of
the log does not have.
**Fix:** a socket that is already closed or disconnected
(`WebSocketDisconnect`, or the `RuntimeError` Starlette raises when sending
on a closed socket) is unregistered with no log line. Any other exception is one `ERROR` line in
the JSON shape `db.py` uses for a failed callback (`level`, `msg`, `exc`).
Tests: a vanished socket during a poke writes no log record; an unexpected
exception writes exactly one, and it parses as JSON.

## R2 — minor — a frame may be 16 MiB                                OPEN

**Where:** `services/api/Dockerfile` (the server command)
**Spec:** `04` §7, as extended with this review
**Problem:** Uvicorn's default limit for a WebSocket message is 16 MiB, and
the limit applies before the handler sees the frame — so also to a client
that never authenticates. The largest frame this protocol has is the `auth`
frame, a few hundred bytes.
**Fix:** `--ws-max-size 4096` in the command. No test can reach it through
`TestClient`; the reviewer will send an oversized frame to a real server.

## R3 — minor — five rules that no test holds                        OPEN

**Where:** `services/api/tests/sync/test_socket.py`
**Spec:** `04` §7 and `07` §5.2 (WS row), as extended with this review
**Problem:** five mutants survive.
(a) Letting the failure of one socket abort the poke: the test with a dead
socket passes anyway, because the live socket happened to come first in the
hub's set. (b) Leaving a dead socket in the hub after its poke failed.
(c) An `unregister` that removes nothing, and (d) a route that never
unregisters on close: nothing looks at the hub after a socket is gone.
(e) Accepting any first frame that carries a valid token: the only
non-`auth` first frame tested carries none.
Also, expiry on an inbound frame is only tested with a `ping`, whose `pong`
would have closed the socket anyway.
**Fix:** tests only; the code is right. (a, b) Make the *first* poke
delivery fail, whichever socket it is, and assert that the other socket
gets the poke, that a second mutation attempts no delivery to the failed
one, and that the hub no longer holds it. (c, d) After an authenticated
socket closes, the hub holds no socket for that family. (e) A first frame
`{"type": "ping", "access_token": <valid>}` closes with 4401. And after the
token expires, with the sweep out of the way, a frame that asks for no
answer (`{"type": "noop"}`) closes with 4401.

## Assumptions and questions

- A frame after `auth` that is not a `ping` is ignored; one that is not
  JSON, or is binary, closes with 4401. Accepted: the client sends only
  `ping`.
- A member disabled while connected keeps the socket until the token
  expires, at most fifteen minutes. Accepted: the socket carries revision
  numbers only, and every pull is authenticated again.
- `POST /me/password` updates a member row, so the revision moves, and it
  does not poke. Accepted: nothing a phone shows changes, and the next
  `pong` carries the revision.
- Maintenance moves revisions from another process and cannot poke.
  Accepted: the 30-second `pong` is the safety net `04` §7 describes.
- The tests bound `TestClient`'s receive through a private attribute,
  because it has no timeout of its own. Accepted; it fails loudly if
  Starlette renames it.
