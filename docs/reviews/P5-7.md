`reconcile()` asks `planReminders` for the desired set, leaving out
`kv.suppressed_reminders`. It compares that set with the scheduled
notifications that carry our three prefixes:

- it cancels ids that are not desired, and ids whose `fp` changed;
- it schedules the missing ones, each on its channel;
- it leaves foreign ids alone.

Native writes are serialized, so an older plan cannot overwrite a newer
one. It runs on foreground, after a pull that applied changes (which
also clears the suppressed list), after an enqueue that touches
reminders, and after permission is granted.
